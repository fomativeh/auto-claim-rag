Auto Insurance Claims Glossary (internal cheat sheet)

Common abbreviations
- FNOL: First Notice of Loss (initial intake report)
- DOL: Date of Loss
- POL#: Policy number
- CLM#: Claim number
- Insd: Insured (policyholder)
- Clmt: Claimant (person making the claim, sometimes insured)
- PD: Property Damage (vehicle or other property)
- BI: Bodily Injury
- Tow: Towing
- RO: Repair Order
- Est: Estimate
- Ded: Deductible

Coverage types (high-level)
- Collision: Damage to the covered vehicle from collision with another vehicle or object. Often applies when the insured hits another car, guardrail, pole, etc.
- Comprehensive: Damage not caused by collision, e.g., theft, vandalism, hail, falling objects. Some carriers call it "Other Than Collision".
- Liability: Damage or injury the insured causes to others. For intake notes, "liability only" often means no first-party collision/comprehensive on the insured vehicle.

Police report
- PR# may be written as Police Report, Incident Report, Case No., Report No.
- Not all losses have a police report. Hit-and-run frequently does.

Injury
- Injury may be stated as "no injuries", "denies injury", "minor whiplash", "went to urgent care", "ambulance".
- If unclear, set injury_reported to null.

Money amounts
- Body shop estimates can be written as $2,450.00, 2450, or "2.45k".
- If there are multiple estimates, use the most recent estimate in the document if obvious.
